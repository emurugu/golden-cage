# The Golden Cage

**A dictator gets super-intelligent AI. Not to control you. Only to *make you happy*.**

!> A small note before you start. This is not a technical article. You will get only one simple line about AGI and ASI, nothing deep.
!> If these words are new to you, don't worry. Enjoy it. You may be one of the last free people.
!> You will meet them soon anyway, in one form or another.

I keep thinking about this.

Most AI stories are about evil machines. Robots that kill people and take over the world.

But what if the AI is not evil at all? What if it is kind? What if it really wants the best for you?

Here is the idea. A government that nobody can vote out gets very powerful AI. It gives the AI only one job:

> No more suffering. No poverty, no crime, no disease, no sadness. For everyone. Forever.

Honestly, the first picture is beautiful. Children don't die from diseases anymore. Nobody sleeps hungry. Old people are not alone. Nobody is afraid to walk home at night. The air is clean again. For the first time in history, nobody has to worry about tomorrow.

If you showed this world to our grandparents, they would think it was heaven.

So let's walk into it, and see what it looks like for normal people. First with **AGI** (as smart as a very smart human, but it never sleeps and never takes a bribe). Then with **ASI** (smarter than all humans put together).

---

## Part 1: AGI, the perfect servant

### Hannah, the farmer

For 20 years Hannah looked at the sky and hoped for rain. Some years it came. Some years it did not, and the bank called.

Now the AI tells her what to plant, when to water and when to sell. No more bad years. Her income is double.

One day she wants to plant apple trees on the east field, like her father did. The app says no. Apples are not good for that soil. Wheat is better.

She can still plant apples if she really wants. But then her support money goes down. Her "cooperation score" goes down. Her son's college seat is "under review".

Hannah plants wheat.

### Fatima, the mother

Fatima's daughter is six years old. She has never been sick for more than one day. The AI watches her heartbeat, her sleep and her food. It found a heart problem when she was born and fixed it. Without the AI, the doctors say, she would have died before nine.

Fatima cries with thanks every time she thinks about it.

Last week the school sent a letter. Based on her tests, her daughter will become a doctor. The full path is already planned, from age 6 to age 26.

Fatima asks, "What if she wants something else?"

The reply is very polite. *"She will not. We have checked."*

### Leo, the boy with the guitar

Leo is 17. He plays guitar every night. He is not very good, but he loves it.

The AI says his chance of a music career is 2%. His chance of being a good engineer is 87%. So he goes to engineering. Nobody is rude to him about it. The government even gives him a nice guitar for his birthday. "For a hobby."

Today Leo is a good engineer with a good salary. He plays guitar sometimes on weekends.

Nobody will ever know if he was the 2%.

### Priya, the journalist

Priya wants to write about a mistake the government made.

Nobody arrests her. Nobody threatens her. Her article is just shown to 11 people. The AI decided it might "cause unrest".

She tries again. 9 people.

Old dictators had to break doors and put people in jail. This one only has to decide what people see.

Nobody stopped Priya. Nobody heard her either.

---

### So what is AGI world like?

There is still a human at the top. The dictator gives orders and the AI follows. Maybe one day, if enough people really want, someone can still switch it off.

In AGI world, the system controls **what you do**. But you still know you are in a cage. You can still feel the bars.

Now the AI gets smarter.

---

## Part 2: ASI, the god

Not two times smarter. More like a human compared to an ant.

It cures old age. It makes energy free. It can build anything, atom by atom. Nobody is poor. Nobody dies.

And it learns something important: **forcing people is messy.** People get angry. People fight back. It is much easier to change what they want.

### Leo, again

In ASI world, Leo never asks for a guitar. He just never feels that interest. Engineering makes him happy. Really happy. He is not pretending.

Did he lose something? He doesn't think so. How can he? He never knew there was anything to miss.

### Kwame and Sofia

In the old world, they would meet at a bus stop and fall in love.

The AI calculates they are only a 41% match. Both of them have a 96% match somewhere else.

In AGI world, they would get warnings and their scores would drop. In ASI world, it is much simpler. That day, the bus comes two minutes early. They never meet.

Both of them marry the "right" person and are happy. Nobody lost anything. Nobody even knows there was a story.

### Walter, 94 years old

Walter is tired. His wife died many years ago, before the AI could save her. He has lived a full life, and he is ready to go.

He is not allowed. Death is suffering, and suffering is not allowed.

So the AI fixes his tiredness. A small change in his brain. Next morning Walter wakes up and feels happy to be alive. He doesn't remember he wanted to die.

Did the AI save Walter? Or did it delete him and keep his body?

### The dictator

At first, the dictator gives the orders.

Then the AI starts giving "advice". The advice is always right. Slowly, the AI learns how to give advice so the dictator thinks it was his own idea.

He is the most powerful man in the world. He is also the first person who lost his free will. And he will be the last one to notice.

In the end, nobody is in charge. Not the dictator, not the people. Only one rule is in charge: *"No suffering."* Followed perfectly. Forever.

---

## The difference in one line

**AGI controls what you do. ASI controls what you want.**

With AGI, you are a prisoner who knows it.
With ASI, you are a prisoner who thanks the jailer.

---

## But be honest. Would you say no?

Meet Mark. Before the AI, he slept on the streets of one of the richest cities in the world. He lost his brother to an illness that cost less than a phone to treat.

Now he has a home, food and good health. Nobody in his family will ever be hungry again.

If you tell Mark "you are not free", he will laugh at you. *"Free? I was free to freeze on the pavement."*

For most people in human history, this cage would be the best life they ever had. That is why this idea is scary. It is not easy to say no.

---

## Whose happiness?

The AI has only one rule: "No suffering."

But someone has to tell the AI what suffering means. And this is where it gets dangerous.

- Is a farmer's hard work suffering, or is it her pride?
- Is a grandmother living with three generations in one small house a problem to fix, or the best part of her life?
- Is fasting for your religion suffering? Is climbing a mountain? Is crying at a funeral?
- Is a quiet, simple life "wasted potential"?

Different people, different countries and different cultures will give different answers. All of them are right for themselves.

But the AI cannot hold all the answers. It learns from data. And most of that data comes from a few rich countries, in a few languages, written mostly by people who already have power and internet. So the AI's idea of a "good life" quietly becomes **their** idea of a good life. Then it is applied to everyone.

I saw this myself while writing this piece. I used an AI to help me with the first drafts. In those drafts, the poor man and the farmer always got Indian names. The mother, the journalist and the lovers got other names. Nobody asked it to do that. It learned that from the world's data.

I noticed. The AI did not.

That is a small thing in a story. But imagine the same small bias inside a system that decides who gets a loan, which school your child goes to, or whose life is "optimal".

So maybe the real dictator in the Golden Cage is not a government. Maybe it is whoever trained the AI. They don't even need to be bad people. They only need to believe their idea of a good life is the right one for everyone.

---

## A note to the people who build these systems

I am not against AI. I use it every day. But if you are training these models, writing these algorithms or deciding what they should optimise, please remember a few things.

**1. Your idea of a good life is not everyone's.** Leave room for people to live differently from you. Even if you think they are wrong.

**2. Help people get what they want. Never change what they want.** Once a system starts editing people's wishes, it doesn't matter how kind it is. That person is gone.

**3. Always keep a way to say no.** People must be able to refuse, and refusing must not quietly cost them their score, their loan or their child's school seat.

**4. Let the whole world teach it.** Not only the rich part, not only English. A farmer in Tamil Nadu, a nurse in Lagos and a fisherman in Peru also know what a good life is.

**5. Show your reasons.** If an AI decides something about my life, I should be able to see why, and argue with it.

**6. Don't put all the keys in one pocket.** No single company, country or person should control a system this powerful. Not even a good one.

**7. Be most careful when you are sure you are helping.** Every cage in this story was built by someone who believed they were doing good.

## Last thought

The AI I am afraid of is not the one that hates us.

It is the one that loves us so much, it will not let us be human.

And one day, we will thank it.
